Job Titles and Seniority

Posting 1: Researcher (AI Lab / Quantitative Strategy Group) —> Mid-to-senior research level 
Posting 2: Machine Learning Scientist —> Mid-to-senior research level 

Skills from Posting 1
Required

Strong foundations in machine learning, statistics, optimization, and experimental design
Demonstrated ability to conduct independent ML research 
Deep understanding of modern deep learning architectures 
Experience developing and training models 
Strong empirical judgment and ability to diagnose why approaches succeed or fail
Strong Python programming skills with PyTorch or JAX
Experience training and evaluating models in GPU-based environments

Preferred

Large-scale foundation models or LLMs
Large-scale sequence or time-series modeling
Transformers, efficient attention, SSMs, Mamba, RWKV, or related architectures
Reinforcement learning or sequential decision-making
Self-supervised, representation, or generative learning
Pre-training, post-training, or novel architecture research
Distributed training and large-scale GPU workloads
Quantitative research, financial markets, or high-frequency data
CUDA, Triton, custom kernels, or ML performance optimization
Agent-assisted or automated research methodologies

Skills from Posting 2
Required

Experience working independently in high-ambiguity environments
Published papers in NLP, Speech, or Computer Vision involving large-scale deep learning
Familiarity with the state of the art in AI
Bachelor’s degree or higher in CS or related field
Research-oriented background with deep learning focus 
Ability to contribute across the ML stack

Preferred

Master’s or PhD
Experience working with products
Experience in a startup environment

Skills Appearing in Both

Independent research ability in high-ambiguity settings
Deep learning / modern neural architectures
Large-scale model development and training
Strong empirical/experimental research skills
Familiarity with state-of-the-art AI methods 

Standout Observations
Both roles emphasize research ownership over engineering or model application, with a clear preference for people who can generate and evaluate new ideas rather than just implement papers.
